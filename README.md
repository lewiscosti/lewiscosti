## Featured Projects

### Tactical Portfolio Management System

Offline-first Python research and portfolio-management system combining **portfolio accounting, market data, quantitative analysis, company fundamentals, valuation, macroeconomic research, portfolio risk, historical testing and AI-assisted investment research**.

Built around a point-in-time and auditable architecture, the system tracks the evidence, assumptions and data available at each decision date to reduce problems such as **look-ahead bias, stale inputs and unsupported conclusions**.

The platform includes an immutable portfolio ledger, multi-currency valuation, deterministic risk checks, event studies, historical portfolio replay, scenario analysis, Excel reporting and optional structured AI research roles designed to challenge investment hypotheses while keeping final decisions human-controlled.

Financial calculations, research outputs, AI interpretation and portfolio transactions remain separated to create a **reproducible investment-research workflow with explicit data lineage and auditability**.

### M&A Buy-Side Evaluation — International Paper / DS Smith

Independent buy-side analysis of **International Paper's acquisition of DS Smith**, combining operating modelling, valuation and transaction analysis.

Built a **3-statement operating model and 10-year DCF**, alongside trading comparables and precedent transactions, to assess DS Smith's stand-alone valuation and implied acquisition price range.

Modelled the proposed combination under alternative **debt/equity financing structures**, incorporating acquisition synergies, pro forma financials and transaction assumptions.

Extended the analysis into **EPS accretion/dilution, maximum purchase price and sensitivity modelling**, testing how transaction economics change across different interest-rate, purchase-price and synergy-realisation assumptions.

### Credit Risk & Debt Covenant Stress-Testing Platform

Python-based credit analysis platform combining **Altman Z-Score analysis, Merton structural default modelling and Monte Carlo simulation**.

The application extracts structured financial information from company PDFs using a locally hosted LLM, applies quantitative credit models and stress-tests debt-servicing capacity and covenant headroom under adverse operating and interest-rate scenarios.

It also generates a structured credit memo summarising the borrower's financial position, model outputs, sensitivities and key credit risks.

### Venture Capital Investment Analysis — Natural

Independent VC-style investment analysis of **Natural**, an early-stage AI agent payments infrastructure company.

Developed an **investment memo and 5-year bottom-up operating model** assessing the company's market opportunity, business model, competitive positioning and potential investment returns.

Modelled transaction volumes, take rates, revenue, margins and cash burn across **Bear, Base and Bull scenarios**, incorporating future financing rounds and investor dilution.

Evaluated potential investor outcomes using **MOIC and IRR analysis**, linking operating assumptions and valuation scenarios to the investment case.

The final analysis combines the quantitative model with assessment of **market size, unit economics, competitive positioning, key risks and investment thesis**.

### Local AI Financial Statement & Disclosure Risk Auditor

Local-LLM financial analysis tool designed to identify and classify potential **financial statement, accounting and disclosure risks** within company reports.

The application combines page-aware PDF extraction, structured LLM outputs and Pydantic validation to identify potential red flags while retaining **page-level evidence references** for review.

Results can be exported to Excel for further analysis, creating a structured workflow for reviewing lengthy financial disclosures without relying on cloud-based document processing.
